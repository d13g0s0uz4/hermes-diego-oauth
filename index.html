hermes-diego

hermes-diego is a personal assistant that Diego Souza runs for himself, on his own server. It is not offered to anyone else, and it has a single user: its owner.

With his consent, it reads his Gmail messages and Google Calendar events to summarize them for him. It has read-only access (gmail.readonly and calendar.readonly): it cannot send, change or delete email or events.
